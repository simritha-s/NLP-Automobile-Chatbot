AUTOCARE SERVICE BOT - TABBED UI DEMO GUIDE (CA4)
==================================================
Run:  pip install streamlit pandas
      python -m streamlit run app.py

Everything (banner, "how it works" graphic, icons) is drawn in code, so
app.py is the only file you need.


HEADER + SIDEBAR
----------------
- Banner image at the top (SVG drawn in code).
- Sidebar: 6 quick-question buttons, branch list, "My bookings" count,
  and a New Conversation button.
- Click "Services under 500" -> the answer appears in the Chat tab.
- The "My bookings" count updates whenever a booking is made or cancelled,
  from the chat or from the Book Now tab.


TAB 1 - CHAT  (intents, entities, multi-turn, context)
------------------------------------------------------
- Open "How it works" for the 3-step graphic.

Booking in chat
  The bot collects the same details as the Book Now form, in this order:
  vehicle, service, date, time slot, branch, name, phone number.
- Try: I want to book a service -> I have a Royal Enfield bike ->
       battery check -> monday -> morning -> mg road -> Sam Kumar ->
       9876543210
  The booking appears under "My bookings" in the Book Now tab, with the
  name, phone number and time slot filled in (marked "booked via Chat").
- Try: what time do you open -> monday -> yes -> car -> car wash ->
       afternoon -> koramangala -> Priya -> 9123456780
  (the date from the availability check is carried into the booking)
- Shortcuts: "tomorrow morning" answers the date and time slot together.
  A clock time such as "3:30 pm" also works and is matched to a slot.
  Names can be typed plainly ("Sam") or as "my name is Sam".
  A phone number may include spaces, dashes or +91.
- Validation (same rules as the form): the time must fall between 9 AM and
  7 PM, the phone number must be exactly 10 digits, and branches are
  closed on Sundays. Try "sunday", "8 pm" or "12345" to see the bot ask again.

Cancelling in chat
- Try: cancel my booking -> 1 -> yes
  The bot lists your bookings and asks which one (by number, or by date such
  as "cancel monday" or "cancel booking 2"). It then asks yes/no before
  cancelling. The booking disappears from "My bookings" and the sidebar
  count goes down.
- With no bookings it replies "You don't have any bookings to cancel."

Interrupting a booking
- In the middle of a booking, click any sidebar question (or type something
  like "reviews for oil change"). The bot answers the new question instead of
  repeating the booking question. Typed interruptions add a note that the
  booking is on hold; say "book a service" to start again.


TAB 2 - SERVICE CATALOGUE  (product catalogue, search, info, filtering)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Search box: wash, tyre, battery
- Vehicle dropdown: choose bike -> AC Service disappears
- Price slider: 0-500 shows the cheapest services
- Sort dropdown: price or duration
- A search with no matches shows a warning


TAB 3 - BOOK NOW  (input boxes and validation)
----------------------------------------------
- Name, 10-digit phone, vehicle, service, date picker, time slot, branch.
- The Service list only offers services that suit the chosen vehicle.
- Try an empty name, a short phone number, or a Sunday date to see the
  validation messages.
- A valid booking shows a confirmation, clears the form, and is listed under
  "My bookings" with a Cancel button.
- Bookings made in the Chat tab are listed here too. Cancelling from either
  place (this button or the chat) removes the booking and updates the
  sidebar count.


TAB 4 - CUSTOMER REVIEWS  (review analysis + sentiment)
-------------------------------------------------------
- Filter by service and branch.
- Metrics + bar chart show Positive / Negative / Neutral counts.
- Each review carries a colour-coded sentiment label.
- "Try the sentiment analyzer": type your own review, e.g.
    Quick service and friendly staff, great value.
  It shows the label and exactly which positive/negative words were matched.
